# Pattern Day Trader Rule Change (2026)

## Summary

FINRA's Pattern Day Trader (PDT) rule was **repealed effective June 4, 2026**, replaced with new intraday margin standards.

## Old Rule (2001 - June 2026)

- Definition: 4+ day trades in 5 business days in a margin account
- Requirement: Minimum $25,000 equity balance
- Restriction: Accounts under $25k limited to 3 day trades per rolling 5-day period
- Penalty: 90-day trading restriction if exceeded

## New Rule (Effective June 4, 2026)

- PDT designation eliminated
- Replaced with **intraday margin standards**
- Brokers have 18-month phase-in period until **October 20, 2027**
- No fixed dollar minimum for day trading
- Brokers set their own intraday margin requirements

## Implications for Small Accounts

- Accounts under $25,000 can now day trade without PDT restrictions
- T+1 settlement still applies for cash accounts
- Margin accounts subject to new (broker-specific) intraday margin requirements
- Pattern day trading restrictions no longer exist
