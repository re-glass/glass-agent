# Pattern Day Trader (PDT) Rule Repeal — Full Timeline

## Summary
The PDT rule, in effect from 2001 to June 2026, required $25,000 minimum equity for pattern day traders in margin accounts. It has been repealed and replaced with new intraday margin standards.

## Timeline

| Date | Event |
|------|-------|
| Feb 27, 2001 | SEC approves PDT rule (amending NASD Rule 2520) |
| 2001–2026 | PDT rule limits day trading for accounts under $25k |
| May 2024 | Settlement cycle changes from T+3 to T+1 |
| January 2026 | FINRA proposes repealing PDT rule |
| April 2026 | SEC approves repeal |
| **June 4, 2026** | **Repeal takes effect** |
| October 20, 2027 | Full implementation deadline for brokers (18-month phase-in) |

## What Replaced It
- **New intraday margin standards** (exact details vary by broker)
- Broker-specific implementation during transition period
- No more universal $25k day-trading minimum

## Key Source
Wikipedia — Pattern day trader (accessed 2026-09-11):
> "In January 2026, FINRA proposed scrapping the rule and replacing it with new intraday margin standards. In April, the SEC approved the proposal, effective June 4, 2026. Brokers that need more time to implement the rule change may phase in their implementation over 18 months, until October 20, 2027."

## Agent Lesson
When giving compliance advice, always verify the current regulatory status. Stating outdated rules as fact erodes user trust and can lead to incorrect trading decisions. When uncertain, say "I believe X, but confirm with your broker" rather than stating it definitively.
